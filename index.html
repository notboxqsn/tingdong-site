常见问题

需要联网吗?

翻译和朗读不需要。语音识别在 iOS 26 及以上完全本地;更早的系统有网时用苹果服务器识别,没网时自动用本地模型。第一次使用某种语言时需要联网下载语言包,设置里有「一键下载全部语言包」。

AI 总结为什么不能用?

AI 总结使用苹果的本地模型,需要支持 Apple Intelligence 的 iPhone(iPhone 15 Pro 或更新)和 iOS 26 以上。不支持的手机其他功能照常。

对方怎么选自己的语言?

倒转那半屏顶部有三个常用语言按钮和一个地球图标,对方点一下自己的语言即可。常用按钮会按地区和最近使用自动排。

我的对话会被上传吗?

不会。对话记录、照片和总结只存在这台手机上,删除 App 即全部删除。详见隐私政策。

FAQ

Does it need internet?

Not for translation or speech. Speech recognition is fully on-device on iOS 26 and later; older systems use Apple's servers when online and the on-device model when offline. Language packs are downloaded once; Settings has a one-tap download for all of them.

Why is AI summary unavailable?

It uses Apple's on-device model and needs an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence (iPhone 15 Pro or newer) on iOS 26 or later. Everything else works on any supported phone.

Is anything uploaded?

No. Conversations, photos and summaries stay on this phone and are removed when the app is deleted. See the privacy policy.
